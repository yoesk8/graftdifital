Sustainable by Design

From seed to skip, we minimise our environmental impact at every step. All green waste is composted or chipped on-site, we use peat-free composts as standard, and our planting designs prioritise native and pollinator-friendly species.

  • Peat-free composts and responsible sourcing
  • Native and pollinator-friendly planting design
  • All green waste composted, nothing to landfill
  • Water-wise irrigation and rainwater harvesting
  • Electric tools where operationally possible
  • Wildlife habitats — log piles, bee hotels, bird boxes
