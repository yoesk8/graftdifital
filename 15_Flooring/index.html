Room by Room

The Right Floor for Every Room

Not sure which flooring suits each space? Here's our quick guide — or book a free home visit and we'll advise in person.

Living Room

Recommended: Engineered Wood or LVT

High-traffic but comfort is key. Engineered wood adds warmth and value; LVT offers practical beauty.

Bedroom

Recommended: Carpet

Carpet is the top choice for warmth underfoot and noise reduction. Saxony and twist piles are perennial favourites.

Kitchen

Recommended: LVT or Sheet Vinyl

Waterproof, easy-clean surfaces are essential. LVT click flooring is especially popular for modern kitchens.

Hallway

Recommended: LVT or Engineered Wood

Your hallway takes the most punishment. Hard-wearing, scratch-resistant LVT with a durable finish is ideal.

Bathroom

Recommended: LVT or Sheet Vinyl

100% waterproof flooring is non-negotiable. LVT tiles give a premium look; sheet vinyl offers seamless coverage.

Stairs & Landing

Recommended: Carpet or LVT

Carpet is the safest and most popular choice for stairs. Hard-wearing twist piles with stair rods look stunning.
