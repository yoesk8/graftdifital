FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How much does an EICR certificate cost?
EICR prices depend on the number of circuits in your property. A typical 1-2 bed flat starts from £120; a 3-4 bed house from £180. We’ll give you a fixed price before we visit — no hidden extras.
Do I need a new consumer unit (fusebox)?
If your fusebox uses old-style rewireable fuses or doesn’t have RCD protection, it likely needs upgrading. All new consumer units we fit use dual RCD protection and meet current BS 7671 regulations.
How fast can you respond to emergencies?
Our 24/7 emergency line is always answered. We aim to attend within 60 minutes across all London boroughs. For central London we’re often there in under 30 minutes.
What is Part P and do I need it?
Part P of the Building Regulations requires that most electrical work in homes is notified to your local council. As NICEIC-registered contractors, we self-certify all notifiable work and issue your certificate directly — you don’t need to contact the council.
Can you install an EV charger at my home?
Yes — we’re OZEV-authorised installers. We can install home EV chargers compatible with all major vehicles. We also assist with the Charge Point Grant scheme to reduce your installation cost.
Are your electricians insured?
Yes. We carry £5 million public liability insurance on every job. All our engineers are NICEIC-registered with valid ID cards. You can verify our registration directly on the NICEIC website before we visit.
