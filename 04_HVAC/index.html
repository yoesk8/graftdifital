Common Questions

Yes — we work across both sectors. Residential projects include air conditioning for homes, flats and apartments, heat pumps, and MVHR ventilation. Commercial work spans offices, healthcare, hospitality, retail and industrial premises.
All of our engineers hold Category I F-Gas certification, which is a legal requirement for handling refrigerants used in air conditioning and heat pump systems. We are also REFCOM registered, the industry-recognised scheme for refrigerant handling.
A single-room split air conditioning system typically takes 4–8 hours. Multi-room or VRF systems for commercial premises can take anywhere from 2 days to several weeks depending on the scale. We’ll give you a detailed programme of works before starting.
Yes — we offer Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) contracts covering annual, bi-annual and quarterly service visits. These keep your systems operating at peak efficiency, maintain warranty validity and are often a compliance requirement for commercial premises.
We install and service all major brands including Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Fujitsu, Samsung, LG and Panasonic. We’ll recommend the best brand and model for your specific requirements and budget.
We cover all London boroughs as well as the surrounding Home Counties. Our engineers are based across the city to ensure fast response times for both planned and reactive work. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.
