Our Services

Everything Your Pool Needs

From initial concept sketches to ongoing maintenance, we handle every aspect of your pool project with unmatched expertise.

Design & Build

Full-service pool design and construction. Our architects and engineers create bespoke pools tailored to your space, style and budget.

  • 3D design visualisation
  • Planning permission support
  • Structural engineering
  • Full project management
  • Interior finishes & tiling
  • Equipment installation

Refurbishment

Breathe new life into an existing pool. Whether cosmetic updates or full structural overhauls, we restore pools to their former glory and beyond.

  • Shell resurfacing & retiling
  • Equipment upgrades
  • Heating system replacement
  • Filtration modernisation
  • Lighting & automation
  • Surround & landscaping

Maintenance & Servicing

Keep your pool in pristine condition year-round. Our dedicated service team provides scheduled visits, chemical balancing and emergency callouts.

  • Weekly/fortnightly visits
  • Water chemistry management
  • Filter servicing & cleaning
  • Seasonal opening & closing
  • Emergency callout service
  • Annual equipment checks
