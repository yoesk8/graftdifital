How It Works

From First Call to Finished Roof

A straightforward, transparent process — so you always know exactly what to expect.

1

Free Roof Inspection

We come to you at a time that suits — one of our experienced roofers will thoroughly inspect your roof and identify every issue, no matter how small.

2

Written Quote — No Hidden Costs

You receive a detailed written quote within 24 hours, breaking down every element of the work and materials. No surprises when the invoice arrives.

3

Work Scheduled at Your Convenience

We plan the job around you. Most repairs are completed the same week — larger projects are scheduled with a full timeline so you’re always informed.

4

Expert Installation & Clean-Up

Our team works cleanly and efficiently. We remove all old materials, leave your property tidy and respect your neighbours throughout the job.

10-Year Guarantee Issued

Once complete, you receive a signed 10-year workmanship guarantee — and we’re always available if anything ever needs attention.
