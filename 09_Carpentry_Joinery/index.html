Our Approach

Built by Hand. Built to Last.

At Grain Carpentry, we believe that furniture and joinery should outlast the people who commission it. Every piece we make is engineered for longevity — hand-fitted, hand-finished, with traditional joinery techniques married to modern precision.

We don't use flat-pack components. We don't rush. We take the time to understand your space, your style and your needs before a single piece of timber is cut.

  • Fully bespoke — designed and built to your exact measurements
  • Choice of any timber species, stain or painted finish
  • Traditional joinery techniques: dovetail, mortise & tenon
  • Soft-close hardware, integrated lighting and power options
  • 10-year structural guarantee on all fitted joinery
